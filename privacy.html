Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how VoiceAI may collect, use, store and protect information when you use our website and AI services.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you create or use a VoiceAI account, we may collect information necessary to provide the service.

  • Name and account information.
  • Email address and authentication information.
  • Profile information you choose to provide.
  • Projects, prompts and generated content.
  • Usage information related to AI tools.
  • Billing or subscription information handled through payment providers.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to operate, maintain and improve VoiceAI.

  • Authenticate your account.
  • Provide requested AI services.
  • Save projects and generations.
  • Apply usage limits and subscription features.
  • Process support requests.
  • Prevent abuse, fraud and unauthorized activity.
  • Improve reliability and user experience.

3. AI Providers

VoiceAI may use third-party AI providers to perform requested processing. Depending on the feature, this may include text-to-speech, speech recognition, image generation, music, sound effects or other AI processing services.

Content sent to a provider is processed according to the provider's applicable terms and privacy policies.

4. Payments

Paid subscriptions may be processed through third-party payment providers such as Stripe. VoiceAI does not intentionally store complete payment card numbers on its own application servers.

5. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect account information and application data.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. You should use a strong password and protect your account credentials.

6. Cookies and Storage

VoiceAI may use browser storage, cookies or similar technologies for authentication, preferences, sessions and application functionality.

7. Information Sharing

We do not intend to sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate requested features, comply with legal obligations, or protect the service and its users.

8. Your Content

Content you upload or generate through VoiceAI may be stored so that requested features such as projects, history and generation management can work.

Do not upload content that you do not have permission to use.

9. Account Controls

You may update available profile information through your account settings. Depending on the implementation, you may also request deletion or correction of account information.

10. Children's Privacy

VoiceAI is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. If you believe a child has provided information improperly, please contact us.

11. Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when our services, technology or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page.
